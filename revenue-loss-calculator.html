Four numbers, one minute. See how many patients you lose after the first visit and what that is worth in shillings every month.

How the calculation works

Patients who never return = new patients per month × (100% − return rate). Each of them would have made the stated number of visits a year at your average bill. "If 10% more came back" means ten more of every hundred new patients returning, which is the kind of improvement consistent reminders and follow-up can realistically aim for. It is an estimate for planning, not a guarantee, and it ignores the bigger prize: the patients who stay also bring their families and their next pregnancy.

What to do with your number

  • Check your real return rate from last month's register. The six retention formulas take about an hour.
  • Start with the service where one lost patient costs the most. For most facilities that is ANC: a mother lost after one visit is up to seven ANC visits, a delivery, PNC and a year of immunisations.
  • Put one person in charge of calling overdue patients every morning.
  • Send reminders patients answer. Use our free English and Kiswahili templates.
