Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

Introduction

HealthBridge Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us in any way.

By accessing our website or using our services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described in this policy, please do not use our website or services.

Our Registration with the ODPC

HealthBridge Solutions is registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya as both a Data Controller and a Data Processor under the Data Protection Act, 2019, under registration number 285-4468-9C40. The Data Controller registration is valid from 19 May 2026 to 19 May 2028 and the Data Processor registration from 26 May 2026 to 26 May 2028.

When we run follow-up programmes for a hospital, the hospital is the data controller for its patients and HealthBridge Solutions acts as its data processor under a written Data Processing Agreement. We process patient data only on the hospital's documented instructions, in line with the Act's principles of lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation and storage limitation.

No automated message is sent to a patient unless her consent has been recorded by the hospital. Patients may opt out of messaging at any time by replying STOP, and may request access to, correction of or deletion of their data through their hospital or directly via our data deletion page.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our healthcare automation services
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries, send updates, and provide customer support
  • Account Management: To manage your account and provide access to our services
  • Marketing: To send promotional materials and information about our services (with your consent)
  • Analytics: To analyze website usage and improve user experience
  • Security: To protect our systems, detect fraud, and ensure compliance
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our business
  • Business Partners: With partners who help us deliver our services
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With Your Consent: When you have given us explicit permission to share your information

Data Security

We apply technical and organisational measures proportionate to the sensitivity of health data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest, provided by our infrastructure providers
  • Role-based access: hospital staff see only their own facility's patients, and access is limited to named, authenticated users
  • Confidential processing mode on every automation that handles identifiable patient data, so message contents are not retained in automation logs
  • An audit trail recording every change to patient records, with the time and the system or user responsible
  • Documented breach response, including notification to the ODPC and affected hospitals within the timelines set by the Act

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep these measures under review and will update them as the service grows.

Data Retention

Patient data (hospital programmes). Identifying details of a mother enrolled in a maternal and child health programme, namely her name, phone number, message replies and nurse notes, are automatically removed 580 days after her recorded delivery date. This period covers the full postnatal and child immunisation schedule to 18 months, plus a short buffer for final follow-up and reporting. An automated purge runs weekly and every removal is logged. After purging, only de-identified records such as visit dates and attendance status are kept, for the hospital's statutory reporting.

If a mother opts out, messaging stops immediately and her record is closed. A hospital may ask us to delete a patient's record earlier, and we will do so unless the hospital is required by law to keep it.

Business contacts and website enquiries. We keep this information for as long as we have a business relationship with you, and for up to two years after our last contact, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict the processing of your information
  • Portability: Request that we transfer your information to another organization
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your information for certain purposes
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section below.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you and remember your preferences.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children. Our maternal and child health programmes do, however, process limited information about infants: the baby's name, date of birth and immunisation dates. This information is provided by the mother as the child's parent and is used only to send immunisation reminders and to produce the child's immunisation record for the family.

Where a mother enrolled by a hospital is under 18, the hospital is responsible for obtaining consent in the manner required by section 33 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 before enrolment.

International Data Transfers

Our service runs on established cloud providers, some of which store or process data outside Kenya. These are: Google (data storage and hospital user interface), Make.com (workflow automation), Vercel (report generation), and Meta (WhatsApp message delivery). Monthly reports are drafted with the help of Anthropic's AI service, which receives aggregated counts only and never patient names, phone numbers or message contents.

We use these providers under their data processing terms, which include contractual safeguards for international transfers, as permitted under sections 48 to 50 of the Data Protection Act, 2019.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
